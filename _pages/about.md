Hi! I'm Clemente Romano, a third-year PhD student in mathematics at NYU's Courant Institute. My advisor is [Prof. Petter Kolm](https://math-finance.cims.nyu.edu/team/petter-n-kolm/).

My research interests span machine learning and quantitative finance, with a particular focus on signature methods. My current projects explore signature-based approaches to stochastic filtering and optimal trade execution.

## Background

I studied mathematics at the University of Naples Federico II, where I completed a bachelor's thesis on Sobolev spaces under the supervision of Prof. Nicola Fusco. I continued my mathematical training through the joint program at the University of Trieste and SISSA.

Before moving into quantitative finance, my interests centered on mathematical analysis, including harmonic analysis, geometric measure theory, the calculus of variations, and partial differential equations.

## Selected work

### Signature-based optimal execution

A Python framework using path signatures and symbolic shuffle algebra to construct and solve quadratic optimal-execution objectives.

[Explore the repository](https://github.com/clementeromano/signature-optimal-execution)

### Kalman filtering with signatures

A numerical reproduction exploring how linear signature regression can recover the continuous-time Kalman-Bucy filter.

[Explore the repository](https://github.com/clementeromano/kalman-filter-with-signatures)

### Prediction-market arbitrage research

ATP tennis data collection and cross-exchange arbitrage research using Polymarket and Kalshi.

[Explore the repository](https://github.com/clementeromano/Polymarket-Kalshi_arbitrage)
